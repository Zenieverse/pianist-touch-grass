# Outdoor Missions Library

## Mission Catalog

### 1. 60-Second Music Walk (Signature Mission)
- **Objective**: Step outside, put the phone away, listen for 60 seconds, and return to translate what you heard into music.
- **Skills**: Active listening, auditory memory, environmental tempo deduction.
- **Reward**: 75 Outdoor XP + Active Listening milestone badge.

---

### 2. Rhythm Hunt
- **Objective**: Locate a repeating physical or mechanical rhythm in your outdoor surroundings.
- **Examples**:
  - Footsteps on gravel / pavement
  - Bicycle chain clicks / wheel rotations
  - Rainfall tapping on leaves or glass
  - Traffic signal beeps
  - Distant construction hammering
- **Musical Transfer**: Convert the detected cadence into an ostinato left-hand bass pattern or steady quarter-note accompaniment.

---

### 3. Melody Hunt
- **Objective**: Detect naturally occurring pitch contours.
- **Questions**:
  - Did the sound move higher, lower, or stay level?
  - What interval did it trace (a step, skip, or leap)?
- **Examples**:
  - Songbirds (robin, sparrow, chickadee)
  - Distant sirens or train horns
  - Wind chimes
  - Human vocal inflections
- **Musical Transfer**: Right-hand 3-note to 5-note melodic reproduction exercise on treble keys.

---

### 4. Silence Mission
- **Objective**: Practice 60 seconds of phone-free, focused auditory presence.
- **Reflection**:
  - What sound did you notice first?
  - What sound repeated?
  - What sound was closest vs. farthest?
  - Was there a natural rhythm?
- **Pedagogical Goal**: Trains the ear to filter noise and focus on spatial acoustic dynamics before playing piano.

---

### 5. Sound Map
- **Objective**: Coarse acoustic classification of the walking environment.
- **Attributes**:
  - Sound Category
  - Loudness (whisper / moderate / loud)
  - Distance (near / mid / far)
  - Repetition (periodic vs. ambient flow)
- **Privacy Notice**: Operates without persistent GPS tracking.
