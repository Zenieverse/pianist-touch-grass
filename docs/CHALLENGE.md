# The Independent Pianist Challenge

## Overview

The **Independent Pianist Challenge** is the ultimate pedagogical North Star of the platform.

Most piano applications reward mechanical note-matching: users match falling colored bars with on-screen keys. When the app is closed, the learner remains dependent and unable to play independently.

PIANIST tests the opposite: **Can you hear an unfamiliar melody with zero visual assistance and reproduce it?**

---

## 🏆 The Ultimate Milestone Flow

1. **Acoustic Presentation**:
   - The learner hears a 7-note musical motif (`E4 - G4 - A4 - G4 - E4 - D4 - C4`).
   - No sheet music notation is displayed.
   - No keyboard keys are highlighted.

2. **Starting Pitch Discovery**:
   - The learner hums or sings the starting pitch and searches on the physical keyboard.
   - The test unlocks Step 2 upon finding `E4`.

3. **Melodic Reproduction**:
   - The learner plays all 7 notes in sequence from auditory memory.
   - Evaluates pitch accuracy and relative interval recognition.

4. **Harmonic Deduction & Transposition**:
   - The learner identifies the underlying functional harmony (`I - V`, C Major and G Major).
   - The learner applies transposition to a new key.

5. **Certification**:
   > **🎉 YOU ARE AN INDEPENDENT PIANIST**
   > *"You didn't memorize this song. You understood it."*
